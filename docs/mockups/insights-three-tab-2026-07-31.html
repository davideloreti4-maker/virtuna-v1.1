design notes
  • The distribution rail replaces “↓60%” as text. Forty-one ticks — every post you’ve made, placed by reach, this one in coral. “You are here” lands in a glance, and it looks like an instrument rather than a stat.
  • Amber earns its place. Rev 2 rendered every good number in grey, which is why it read flat. Amber is the cortex’s own dorsal-attention hue, so the palette is derived from the product’s model rather than picked off a shelf. Coral stays loss-only.
  • Cards, and a type scale with actual steps — 52 / 36 / 26 / 20 / 15 / 13 / 11. Rev 2 had six sizes between 12.5 and 14.5px.
  • The drop-off ledger folded into the curve. Exit counts now sit as coral ticks on the retention line where they happen — one section removed, one fewer bar chart.
  • The unlock moved up and got a surface of its own — warm gradient, amber rule. It was the best card on the page and it was last.

design notes
  • “The room” is “your audience” throughout — tab, hero, ledgers, trust card. Room was internal vocabulary that had leaked onto the surface.
  • Form variety. Rev 2 answered every question with a bar. Traffic is now a donut, gender a gauge, age columns, retention sparklines, regions bars — each question gets the shape that suits it, which is what TikTok does.
  • Hover a retention pool and its district lights on the map above. The two figures were describing the same people with no visible link.
  • When to post — the engine already models OptimalPostWindow. A time recommendation with a reason is pure cheat-code and it was sitting unused.
  • Demographics side by side rather than stacked, so the card is one glance instead of two scrolls.
