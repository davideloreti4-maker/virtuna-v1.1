Ambient Audience v2 · detail drill · rev 4

Role-shaped tabs. The figures swap; the questions don’t.

Three tabs, two instruments. A video sim has a timeline and no voices; a text sim has real coded reasons and verbatim voices and no timeline. Rev 1–3 designed a video screen and greyed the slots text couldn’t fill. Here each tab holds an invariant question and swaps the figure that answers it — so a text sim reads as a different instrument, not a degraded one. One accent throughout: coral is loss, cream is everything that is working.

Design notes — what changed from rev 3, and why

The swap table

Each tab is a question. The figure that answers it is chosen by StimulusKind. This is the domain-template.ts contract that already exists — BrainDriver is a union with attention-scrubber and reason-breakdown in it, and rev 1–3 simply never used the seam.

Both columns are full. Neither borrows the other’s figure, and nothing is greyed out to stand in for a producer that doesn’t exist. The only dashes in the whole design are the three sensory rows in the text deeper-read, which are dashed rather than dimmed because a hook has no footage — an absence with a reason, printed once, inside a collapsed section.

Answering the four rejection points

  • One accent. Coral appears on loss only — a below-median projection marker, the pool that drops first, the objection weights, the “gone” row. Positive states are cream at full strength, and rank is carried by weight, size and position: the hero number is 40px light, section reads are 13px dim, notes are 10.5px mono. No amber, no second hue anywhere.
  • Matte. No gradient, no shadow, no blur, no inset-shine. Depth comes from four cream alphas (.62 / .38 / .16 / .08) and one recessed tone (#262624), which is the whole shipped system.
  • Connected surface. No card class exists in this file. Sections are a hairline plus 30px of rhythm — the grammar the shipped Unlock atom already uses. The only bordered objects are the gain chip and the filmstrip cells, both of which are chips, not containers.
  • Five forms, not eleven. The line, the bar-row, the segmented stack, the terrain, the quote. The room mix is a single segmented bar instead of a donut; retention-by-pool is the same line form repeated four times instead of four chart types. Repetition is what makes 440px read calm.

Where serif earns its place

Newsreader is reserved for one thing: a voice from the room. That means it appears in the text sim’s comment wall and nowhere else in the drill. It is the clearest signal that a text sim is a different instrument rather than a video screen with holes — video is numbers and a timeline, text is counts and human sentences.

Open decisions, answered

  • Text tab set — same three roles, swapped figures. One template object per domain, no page fork.
  • Where the verbatim wall lives — The reaction. It is what they’d say, which is the same question video answers with intents. The coded breakdown stays on The read because it is the mechanism, not the response.
  • Two stop numbers — printed with their own denominators and never adjacent. Weighted hook attention rides the persistent readout; the headcount rides the ledger; the trust strip states plainly that they are different measures.
  • The cortex — The read only, and only on video, at 46px, riding the instrument legend as a readout glyph. It is driven by the retention value under the playhead, which is what cortex-sim.ts’s unused grounded mode already does.
  • Benchmark bands — held. Every comparison in this design is against the creator’s own catalogue, which needs no external calibration to be true.
