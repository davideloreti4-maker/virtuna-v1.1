Ambient Audience v2 · detail drill · rev 5

The answer first, then the evidence.

Rebuilt in a product idiom: soft filled cards instead of hairline rules, a segmented control, chips carrying the metadata, and icons on every card header. The verdict now rides a persistent answer block above the tabs, so the thing you paid for is never hidden behind one. Coral still means loss and nothing else — active states are cream.

Design notes — what changed from rev 4, and why

The idiom

Rev 4 grouped with hairline rules and labelled everything in uppercase mono. That reads terminal, not product. Rev 5 groups with soft filled cards (#212120 on a #181817 rail, 14px radius, no border) and labels with sentence-case 13px medium plus a line icon. Metadata moved into chips. Tabs became a segmented control. Numbers went from hairline-thin-and-huge to medium-weight-and-confident, which is what every shipped AI product actually does.

Two adaptations that were forced

  • Perplexity spends its accent on positive states — links, active tabs, icons are all cyan. Virtuna's law is coral = loss only, so active states here are cream on a lighter fill (#343330) and coral never marks a good thing. The palette stays at one accent.
  • The rev-3 ban on card-in-card still holds, but the failure there was borders inside borders. Cards here group by fill and go exactly one level deep — inside a card you get rows, chips and charts, never another card. The only nested fills are chips, which read as objects rather than containers.

The structure

The verdict was previously buried inside a tab. It now rides a persistent answer block above the segmented control: a plain-language sentence, the numbers that support it, and the fix as a tappable chip. Whatever tab you're on, the answer and the action stay on screen. Tabs are then just lenses on the evidence.

The figures still swap per kind

This part of rev 4 was never the problem and is preserved: StimulusKind is hook|video|idea|draft, and only video has a timeline. Video and text are inverse instruments, so each tab holds an invariant question and swaps the figure that answers it.
