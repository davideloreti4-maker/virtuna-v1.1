Numen Machines · sketch, not a build

Maven — the product shape

Every surface, in order of how a creator meets them. Built against the locked design system (globals.css): flat-warm charcoal, cream text, matte, and the accent at near-zero dosage — at most one accent element per frame, zero on most. Primary actions are neutral cream, never accent.

Decision this rests on: the evidence layer is a curated, shared, structural format library — see docs/DECISION-outlier-corpus-2026-08-07.md. Tiles are formats, not videos in your niche.
Seeded from what already exists: the 532-row Sandcastles corpus (public.outlier_teardowns) — human-curated, classified into 13 hook archetypes, with rank.ts already selecting round-robin across archetypes ("six examples, six ways to open"). That is the Board's selection rule, already written. Measured 2026-08-07: 211 rows are printable (≥3×, under the absurd tail) and they cover all 13 archetypes — see §12.
Domain is never shown. Naming the donor domain manufactures an objection the user didn't have. The cross-domain machinery stays invisible.
Chat stays the main surface (owner call). The Board is the thread's arrival state, not a replacement for it — see §11.
⚠️ Read the scope before costing this

This document shows every surface a creator touches, so it looks like a redesign. It is not. Almost everything below already exists and is untouched: the skills, the decode → adapt → SIM chain, the thread and its block registry, Work, Audience, credits and the walls. Four things actually change:

1 · The arrival stateLargely a reinstatement — see §14
2 · The thread's render treatmentResults get more weight. No data change.
3 · The audience presenceNew concept — the front row, §11
4 · Corpus → Board wiringThe one genuinely new pipe

Plus one gap that must close for any of it to be true: platform never reaches the simulation (§frame 01). Everything else in these frames is reference — how existing parts recompose, not new things to build.

Why this actually worked

The topic is irrelevant. These four beats are the part that travels.
0:00 0:02 · claim 0:07 · the turn 0:14
Hook patternStates a position the viewer already disagrees with, flatly, with no hedge.
StructureClaim → resistance → single piece of evidence → restated claim. No list, no recap.
The turnAt 0:07 the justification arrives and it is narrower than expected — that gap is the retention.
Emotional beatIndignation, then relief. The viewer gets to be wrong safely.
Repeatable — this is what we adapt
  • The flat contrarian claim in the first 2 seconds
  • Withholding justification until 0:07
  • One piece of evidence, never three
Luck — stripped, never carried over
  • Creator already had 400k followers
  • Rode a trending audio for 48 hours
  • Topic was in the news that week
Zero accent on this frame. The luck lane is dashed and dimmed — visually subtracted, which is the point. Maps 1:1 onto DecodeResult: 4 beats + repeatable[] + luck[], with D-01 guaranteeing luck can't reach the adapt prompt. What happens to the thread — and to the rail

The two live pieces this shape displaces. Neither gets deleted; both get redistributed.
Owner call: chat stays the main surface. Three reasons, and they hold — everything is already built around it, users arrive already knowing how a chat works, and it supports the positioning "the ChatGPT for social media creators," which is instantly understood and therefore cheap to market. That last one is a real GTM asset that a pure interaction-efficiency argument doesn't weigh.
So the Board is not a replacement for the thread — it is the thread's arrival state. ChatGPT and Claude both do exactly this: a composer with suggestions around it. The only difference is that ours aren't generic prompts, they are proven formats carrying a number. Nothing in frames 03–09 is wasted; the Board moves from "home instead of chat" to "what is on screen before the first message," and the Bench stops being a separate mode and becomes how a result renders inside the thread.
Thread does todayAfter
Stores typed blocks in messages, one open thread per userUnchanged. No migration, no schema change, block registry untouched.
Opens on an empty composerOpens on the Board. Receipt + six formats + in progress, with the composer in place. The single non-negotiable: the thread is never blank on arrival.
Skill results render as cards in the scrollSame place, more weight. The decode, the five angles and the vote render as a full-width unit in the thread rather than a compact card — the "Bench" is a render treatment, not a route.
Scroll up to find what you madeWork becomes the durable index. Scrolling still works; it is just no longer the only way back.
Multi-turn steering in the composerBoth. The composer keeps working — that is the familiarity the positioning is built on. A steer field on the card is the shortcut, not the replacement.
The risk that comes with the positioning: "the ChatGPT for X" invites "why not just use ChatGPT?" The answer — proven formats with real numbers, and an audience that votes — has to be visible on the first screen, not discovered in session three. Which is the same conclusion from the other direction: the arrival state carries the whole differentiator.
The rail is not chrome — it is four jobs stacked in a column. AmbientOverviewRail (mounted in the composer at ≥xl, behind AMBIENT_V2_ENABLED) holds a projected-card ledger, a Simulate trigger, a sealed-verdict display, and a depth drill. Killing "the rail" means moving four things, not deleting one.
And the replacement is not "less audience" — it is more. The earlier note in this doc said kill the rail; that conflated presence with real estate. A 280px panel that never moves is furniture. Ten faces that sit quietly and then react are alive. The concept below is more present than the rail at a tenth of the footprint, because presence comes from behaviour.

14

"How does anyone know what this can do?"

If skills leave the front door, capability becomes invisible. This is the strongest objection to the whole shape — and the answer is already written in the repo.
A menu of 13 skills is a worse fix than it looks. A skill list is a list of verbs, and users don't think in verbs — they think in jobs. "Write hooks" is a verb. "I don't know what to post" is a job. Any capability surface has to be phrased as outcomes, or it reads as a manual.
Discoverability should come from adjacency, not from a menu. Every result already carries its next moves — "5 more like this", "turn into a script", "who said no?", the steer field. Users learn the skill graph by walking it. That is how a chat product teaches without a manual, and it needs nothing new.
But adjacency only teaches what's adjacent. It will never surface "Test a real video" to someone who has only ever made hooks. That needs a visible map — which is what the old start page was.

The corpus, measured — not assumed

Queried against public.outlier_teardowns on 2026-08-07. Two findings contradict the docs.
MeasureValue
Rows total532
Carry a multiplier396 — 136 have none at all
Clear the 3× "proven" bar288

Absurd tail46 rows at 50–200×, 31 above 200×, max 20,154× — unprintable, they read as broken

211 printable rows answers the cadence question: yes, a weekly feed works. Six per week is 35 weeks before a single repeat, and that is without rotation, personalisation, or ever adding a row. All 13 archetypes are represented in the printable band, so rank.ts's round-robin still yields six distinct shapes every week.
⚠️ Contradicts the docsWhat the data actually says
follower_countNULL on all 532 rows. So views ÷ followers cannot be computed from this table at all — whatever produced outlier_multiplier, it was not that.
baseline_labelOnly ever "vs their usual views" or NULL — never "vs followers." The session-2 handoff records decision F as owner-confirmed views ÷ followers → baseline_label = 'vs followers'. The data disagrees. Until that is resolved, every tile would print the wrong basis.
This is the corpus handoff's own lesson landing again: the defect was invisible in the source and invisible to the test suite, and took one query to surface. Resolve the basis before any number reaches a tile — and cap the printable band, because a "20,154×" badge destroys more credibility than it buys.

How many rows clear 3×ANSWERED — 288 clear it, 211 are printable, all 13 archetypes covered. See §13. A weekly feed is comfortably supported.
What the multiplier is measured againstOpen and blocking. The docs say views ÷ followers; the data says "vs their usual views" with follower_count NULL on every row. Nothing prints until this is settled.
Cross-domain decode qualityStill the load-bearing assumption, but now cheap to test against real rows: adapt 20 corpus decodes into 3 unrelated domains each and read them.
Saturation controlA shared library means we eventually cause the saturation we detect. Rotation and retirement need designing before launch, not after.
