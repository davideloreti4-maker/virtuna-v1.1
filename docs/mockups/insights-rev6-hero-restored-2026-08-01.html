Calls you may want to reverse

  • The archetypes are gone. builders / learners / skeptics / drive-by named attitudes; the room now splits by relationship to you — followers · returning · new viewers · outside your niche — TikTok’s own vocabulary, one taxonomy across terrain, watch-time, fit and spread. The people are individuals again: each voice carries a human descriptor (“small creator”), not a caste.
  • The read lines are gone entirely (rev 11). Rev 10 anchored every foot to its card’s numbers; the owner still read them as prose. “Or nothing” won: cards end on their data, the verdict headline is the surface’s one sentence, and the answer’s evidence is a stat row.
  • Tab order is Brain · Engagement · Audience. The answer’s evidence is the retention curve, so they sit adjacent. Brain and Engagement are both about the CLIP; Audience is about the ROOM — scope widens left to right instead of interleaving the two subjects.
  • Retention is Engagement’s again (your call, and it holds): it’s what the room DID with the clip second by second, so it belongs with the video that produced it and the outcomes it explains. Brain keeps the cognitive read of the same clip.
  • The instrument left the drawer for the surface. Rev 8 folded it away so the page spoke one unit; the cost was a Brain tab thinner than the shipped one. It’s back, with each card’s right-meta naming its scale — that was the real fix for the unit problem.
  • Grades carry no colour. The shipped rail paints WEAKNESS coral; that file calls the bands “a cutoff on a MODELED signal, not a benchmark against real outcomes”. An unbenchmarked judgement shouldn’t shout — and Brain’s one coral belongs to the loss.
