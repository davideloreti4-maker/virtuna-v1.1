Skill cards v7 · the card becomes an object

a media panel gives the card geography; the face loses half its words

The reframe this round

Six treatments across three rounds all restyled the same medium. Boxes, stacked labels, run-in labels, bold lead-ins, glyphs: every one was a column of sentences, and a column of sentences is a document no matter what the typography does. Your read was right each time.

What a document lacks is geography. The cards that feel like product objects (Perplexity discover, Spotify, YouTube Studio) have a visual anchor and a spatial layout: the eye lands on an image, then reads beside it. Every one of these cards is about a video, so the anchor is sitting right there: show the video. Two ways, one per direction. First frame renders the video you are about to make: a 9:16 panel with your hook set as on-screen caption caps at 0s. Drawn by us, honest as a styling, and it makes the deliverable feel already half-made. Case study leads with the proven source video instead: its cover, the multiplier badged on it, your line beside it. The cheat-code story in one glance.

Both also cut the face word count roughly in half: one sentence per fact, the audience becomes faces plus a short line, the seed line leaves the face (stays on props). On mobile the panel floats beside the hero and the text flows around it, feed-style; there is no two-column text layout anywhere.
