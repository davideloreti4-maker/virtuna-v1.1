Your three notes, and the one fix behind them

All three complaints share a root cause: a 9:16 portrait panel beside text is the wrong geometry. A 263px-tall image column can never be filled by three lines of text, so the text floats in space (your "empty and unstructured"), the image dominates (your "too big"), and when the reference is absent the scaffold collapses (your "even worse"). Perplexity gets away with side images because theirs are landscape.

v9.2 rebuilds the card as four full-width bands: the face (hero and dek, full line length, no compression), the source row (the case study as a proper component: 52px cover, "Case study · @handle" over the stat line, chevron, tappable), the brief row, the footer. Every band shares the same left edge. Remove the source band and the card is still a complete object. And mobile is now literally the same layout as desktop — the cover special-casing is gone entirely.
