The system rule

Choice cards scan; deliverable cards read. Hooks arrive five at a time and ideas four at a time: their job is a pick, so they get the compact four-element face and the staged brief. A script or remix arrives alone and is the thing itself: the beat timeline and the decode map stay on the face, wrapped in the same shell (header, case-study source, one hairline language, footer door). One visual system, two behaviors, matched to what the user is doing.

One change from v9: the idea card's take moved onto the face, inside the dek, because the take is what makes an idea the creator's own and that is decision-relevant at scan time. The mechanism moved to the brief in its place.
