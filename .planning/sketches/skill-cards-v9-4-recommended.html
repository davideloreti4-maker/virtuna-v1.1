Skill cards·v9.4 — the recommendation ·1 Aug 2026

Set the deliverable like a deliverable.

v9.2b's face is half empty at 728 because an eight-word hook line set at 22px cannot fill a 688px column — and the two ways to fill it both failed a constraint: a second column costs 137px on mobile, and narrowing the card only works for cards whose hero is one short line. The third way is to stop shrinking the hero. At 32px the line fills its measure, the card reads as an object with a subject, and nothing changes about the geometry — so mobile is the same layout at 21px. Everything else here is the audit's fixes, integrated: the brief becomes a map, the set gets an axis, the door closes its loop, and the source row tells the truth about its own number.

01 — The seven moves

What changes, and what each one is for

01

The hero scales to the card, not the card to the hero

32px at 728, 21px at 342. The hook line is the product; at 22px it was a caption with a lot of room around it. This is the entire fix for "empty at desktop" and it costs nothing on mobile, because the whole design already narrows rather than re-flows.

Reading: the hero holds a 22ch measure, the dek 70ch — both inside the comfortable band. Nothing gets a 100-character line.

02

Rank comes back, and every card wears one differentiator

#1 through #5, plus the taxonomy the schema already carries — visualHook.technique on hooks, format on ideas. Five cards you can tell apart without opening anything.

No engine work. Both fields exist and are populated today; v9.2b simply stopped rendering them.

03

The brief is a map, not a list

Two columns of labelled cells at 728, one at 342 — the same grid language as the remix decode map, which is the one structured surface in the system that survived review. It escapes the typed-cell document idiom, kills the dead right half, and has room for topic and format.

Mobile cost handled: below 480px the map drops to five cells — the restored topic/format cell folds into Format — so mobile does not pay for the desktop gain.

04

Every right edge is named

Open on TikTok ↗ on the source row, Expand › on the brief. One caret convention, one external glyph. No unexplained gaps, and the chevron stops promising a drill-in and delivering a new tab.

05

The door gets its other two states

Untested → testing → 8 of 10 stopped · See your audience →. The verdict left the face on the promise that the door would own measurement; this is that promise kept. Legacy measured cards land in the third state directly, which also settles the open legacy-door question.

06

The source row states the number it actually computed

● in your audience · 90.7× vs followers · 621K views. The fit glyph returns, the basis label returns, and the "their usual reach" paraphrase goes — it described a ratio nothing in the pipeline calculates.

Row title derives from the data: "Case study" only when a fit label exists; "The post you're remixing" when the user supplied the video and nothing scored it.

07

The footer groups: do it · keep it  ·  test it

Primary and a labelled Save on the left; the door alone on the right. At 342 the door takes its own row under a hairline instead of a bare icon stranded mid-row. One is the chain step, one is a diagnostic — they no longer look like siblings.

Read it honestly — v9.4 buys legibility with height. It is taller than v9.2b almost everywhere: hook +53 at 728 and +29 at 342, idea +74 / +53, script +22 / +51, the open brief +23 at 342. That is the price of a 32px hero and a six-cell map, and against what actually ships today the choice cards are still far shorter — −145 and −161 at 728, −232 and −266 at 342. Remix is the one that improves on v9.2b (−49 at 342) but still sits 56px above the shipped card; v9.2b did not close that regression and neither does this. If mobile height is the binding constraint, the two levers are the map's cell count and the script's per-beat retention line.

Correction, 1 Aug: the first published version of this page understated every 342 number. Its script switched width and measured in the same frame, so it read the stage mid-transition. Fixed here, and cross-checked headlessly with the animation settled — the two methods now agree exactly. Two deliberate content changes are also baked in and should be judged on their merits: the remix "How to film" no longer repeats the shot list the decode map already states, and the brief's sixth cell folds below 480px.

06 — If you only do three

The order I would build in

  1. The hero at 32/21. One line of CSS per choice card. It is the whole "premium at desktop" problem, and it is free on mobile.
  2. Rank plus one taxonomy chip. Restores the set's scannability with fields that are already populated — no engine change, no schema change.
  3. The brief as a map. Escapes the idiom that has now been rejected twice, and reuses the one grid that survived.

The rest — named right edges, the three door states, the honest multiplier, the grouped footer — are small and independent. The two that still need your decision are whether the "Made for" cell ships behind reaction_frame or degrades, and whether "vs followers" is the wording you want users to read.
