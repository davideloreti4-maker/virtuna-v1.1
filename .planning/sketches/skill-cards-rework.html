Skill cards — rework · throwaway sketch · task/skill-cards-rework

The card stopped asking the question the simulation answers.

Every Make card ships a verdict today: a band word, an N/10 count, a magnitude ribbon, a verbatim quote and a #1–#5 rank. All five are renderings of one number — personaStops — which the writer model emits about its own output, in the same call that wrote it. No simulation runs. Nothing is measured.

So the card answers "will this land?" before the creator can ask it, and the tool that would actually answer it — the brain, the population — reads as a re-run of something they already have. The fix is to stop answering: the card carries the artifact, the simulation carries the verdict, and the empty slot between them is the reason to run one.

The contract

Five rows, in order. A card may skip one; it may not reorder them.

01
Meta
One quiet mark left (Top pick / needs your take), one affordance right (Copy). Never provenance.
02
Hero
The deliverable, serif. The thing they came for reads first — a line, a title, an opener.
03
Why — one line
The mechanism, two lines max. Not a labelled section; it sits under the hero as prose.
04
The door
The one slot a verdict may ever occupy. Empty until a real run fills it. This is the new element.
05
Actions
Forward chain step · the ONE expand · Save. Everything else in the card lives behind that caret.
What leaves the face entirely

Band word N/10 fraction magnitude ribbon lead "verbatim" quote room avatars #1–#5 rank badge · SIM-1 Flash / · projected tag — six of these are one number wearing six costumes; the seventh is a provenance footnote for a measurement that never happened.

Nothing is deleted from the block schema. fraction stays on the props — the ambient ledger keys card→room on it (toAmbientDescriptor returns null without it), so removing the field would silently unhook every card from the room. This is a presentation change on top of unchanged data.
